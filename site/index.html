Design System & Architecture

Engineered to Apple Standards.

💎

Apple Liquid Glass Materials

Translucent frosted panels crafted with 32px backdrop blur, 210% saturation boost, dual specular top-edge highlights, and G2 continuous curvature squircles that respond to ambient lighting.

🏝️

Dynamic Island HUD

Floating morphing pill that springs dynamically during combo streaks, level-ups, and life events with real-time particle feedback.

⚡

60 FPS Kinetic Physics

Zero-latency omnidirectional vector physics with momentum damping, speed warp lines, shockwaves, and responsive keyboard + touch controls.

🔊

Procedural Web Audio

Synthesized harmonic oscillators with Apple smooth attack and decay curves. Zero latency, respects system muting instantly.

📦

Zero Dependencies

Ultra-lightweight ~28 KB total bundle. Instantaneous cold boot on mobile, desktop, and embedded WebViews.
